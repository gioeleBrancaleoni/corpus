# PRODUCT.md — Corpus

**What it is:** A local, privacy-first RAG web app. Point it at a folder of documents; browse, preview, download them; ask questions answered by a local Ollama LLM with grounded, clickable citations. No data ever leaves the user's infrastructure.

**Who it's for:** People and small teams with sensitive documents (legal, medical, HR, financial, GDPR-relevant) who want "chat with my documents" without any cloud.

**Register:** product — design serves the task (a reading room for your own documents). Familiarity and trust over surprise.

**Surfaces:** three-pane app shell (Library tree / Viewer+Chat tabs / Sources panel), settings dialog, ingestion progress, empty states.

**Voice:** calm, precise, honest. The privacy promise is stated plainly, never marketed. Errors are specific and actionable ("Ollama not reachable at http://…"), never cute.

**Non-goals:** no marketing pages, no onboarding tours, no accounts, no telemetry.
